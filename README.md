## About the Game

**Quarantine Zone: The Last Check** is a survival simulation game developed by **Brigada Games** and published by **Devolver Digital**. In this intense decision-making experience, you play as a military officer responsible for screening refugees and maintaining a quarantine zone during a zombie outbreak.

### Your Role as the Officer

At the heart of Quarantine Zone lies your responsibility to decide who gets in and who doesn't. Each day, new individuals appear—some desperate, some suspicious, and some possibly infected. You are given tools like thermometers, infection detectors, and UV lights to inspect their health. However, not all symptoms are visible, and not every lie is easy to catch. You will have to make judgment calls without full information, knowing that a single wrong decision could allow an outbreak inside the zone.

### Core Gameplay Mechanics

#### 🔍 See It? Sort It
Use high-tech tools to uncover signs of infection among terrified survivors. Identify contraband, forbidden items, and even rare collectables as you work to keep your checkpoint secure. Send suspicious cases to quarantine, route individuals to the lab for additional screening, save healthy survivors, and deal with infected individuals decisively.

#### ⚡ Command the Catastrophe
As the survivor population swells, you'll be relied upon to provide essentials such as power, food, and medicine. When alarms are raised, pilot armed drones to ensure the infected don't breach the compound. Manage resources, reinforce defenses, and upgrade facilities to maintain order.

#### 🧬 Learn. Adapt. Survive.
When a quarantined survivor becomes an undead predator, all is not lost! Keep newly turned zombies confined to harvest and prepare samples in return for new tools, staff XP, and long-term upgrades. Face fatal testing decisions and choose between preserving life or pursuing knowledge.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What is Quarantine Zone: The Last Check?
It's a survival simulation game where you run a checkpoint in a post-outbreak world. You inspect civilians and manage a base under constant pressure.

### What is the player's role in the game?
You act as a military officer responsible for screening refugees and maintaining the quarantine zone. Every day you decide who to let in, who to isolate, and how to use limited resources.

### How does the inspection system work?
You use tools like scanners, UV lights, and thermometers to evaluate each person. You must also observe behavior and question their story to catch inconsistencies. Our detailed inspection guides cover everything from visual observation to advanced interrogation techniques.

### Are there consequences for wrong decisions?
Yes, mistakes can cause outbreaks, riots, or damage morale. Consequences build over time and change the direction of the game.

### Is there a storyline or campaign mode?
Yes, the game has a branching narrative with multiple possible outcomes. Your choices shape the events and relationships within the quarantine. Navigate moral dilemmas and special events with our Campaign Guide.

### Can you upgrade your base?
Yes, you can invest in medical stations, better scanners, defenses, and quarantine cells. Each upgrade affects how efficiently your base runs. Discover the best upgrade strategies in our base management guides.

### Is combat part of the gameplay?
No direct combat, but you will manage armed units and make security decisions. The focus is on strategy and command, not action.

### How long is a typical game session?
Sessions vary, but a full run through the campaign may take 6–12 hours. Replayability is high due to different choices and outcomes.
